# PetPulse AI - Smart Pet Health & Behavior Monitoring App

PetPulse AI is a comprehensive pet health monitoring app that uses computer vision and AI to analyze pet photos/videos for early health warnings, behavioral changes, and wellness insights. With pet ownership at all-time highs and vet costs skyrocketing, owners desperately need affordable early intervention tools. The app combines symptom tracking, AI-powered photo analysis, and personalized health recommendations to help pet parents catch issues before they become expensive emergencies.

## About

Pet owners spend $4.7 billion annually on vet bills in Australia alone, with emergency visits averaging $1,500-$5,000. The biggest pain is that symptoms often go unnoticed until pets are critically ill - by then treatment costs explode and outcomes worsen. Current solutions are either basic symptom trackers (no AI insights) or expensive wearable devices ($200+ that pets often reject). Pet parents are posting millions of 'is my pet sick?' photos to Facebook groups and Reddit, getting inconsistent advice from unqualified strangers. There's a massive gap for an affordable, AI-powered early warning system that analyzes visual cues, behavioral patterns, and symptoms to flag potential health issues before they require emergency intervention.

## Market Opportunity

Global pet care market is $261 billion and growing at 6.1% annually. Digital pet health specifically is $2.4 billion, growing at 22% yearly. In New Zealand, 68% of households own pets (1.4 million households), spending $1.8 billion annually on pet care. Australia has 29 million pets across 69% of households, with $15 billion annual pet spending. UK pet owners number 17 million households spending £9 billion yearly. The addressable market is massive: if just 5% of NZ/AU pet-owning households paid $15/month, that's $63 million annual revenue opportunity across these markets alone.

## Tech Stack

Core tech stack: React Native app, Python backend, AWS hosting, computer vision APIs (Google Vision, AWS Rekognition, custom models). No proprietary data requirements - can train on open pet health datasets and user-contributed photos. MVP buildable in 2-3 months: photo upload, basic AI analysis, symptom tracking, user profiles. The AI training is the complex part but doable with existing frameworks like TensorFlow/PyTorch. No hardware dependencies or locked APIs. Scalability handled by cloud infrastructure. Main technical risk is AI model accuracy - requires good training data and iterative improvement.

## Point of Difference

Our core differentiator is advanced computer vision AI trained specifically on pet health indicators - skin conditions, eye problems, posture changes, gait abnormalities. We're software-only (no expensive hardware), making it accessible to all pet owners. Unlike basic symptom trackers, we provide proactive AI insights from photos. Unlike expensive vet telehealth ($75+ per consultation), we offer 24/7 automated screening for $15/month. Our behavioral pattern recognition learns each pet's baseline and alerts to deviations. We generate professional vet reports from our analysis, bridging the gap between pet parent concerns and veterinary consultation.

## Target Users

Primary: Urban millennials and Gen X (ages 28-45) who consider pets family members. Household income $60k+, tech-comfortable, proactive about health. Secondary: Empty nesters and seniors with disposable income who want peace of mind. Tertiary: Multi-pet households and pet foster families who need to monitor multiple animals cost-effectively. Also veterinary practices as B2B customers for client engagement and triage.
