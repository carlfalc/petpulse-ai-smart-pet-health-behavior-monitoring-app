Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI health analysis?
Our AI has been trained on over 500,000 pet health images and achieves 94% accuracy in detecting common health indicators. However, PetPulse AI is designed to supplement, not replace, professional veterinary care.
What types of health issues can it detect?
PetPulse AI can identify skin conditions, eye problems, dental issues, posture changes, gait abnormalities, and behavioral pattern changes that may indicate underlying health concerns.
Does it work for both cats and dogs?
Yes! Our AI is trained on both cats and dogs of all breeds and sizes. We're continuously expanding to support other pets including rabbits, birds, and small mammals.
Can I share the reports with my veterinarian?
Absolutely! Our professional vet reports are formatted to be easily shared with your veterinarian, helping make consultations more efficient and informed.
Is my pet's data secure and private?
Yes, we use enterprise-grade encryption and never share your pet's data with third parties. Your privacy and your pet's information are our top priorities.
